`tools/rebuild_dialogue.py` updates both group-descriptor blob offsets and individual reference offsets, then moves downstream sections. Group IDs, reference IDs, untouched row bytes and the suffix remain unchanged. It preserves control order, recognized numeric arguments and percent placeholders. Forty-eight identity rebuilds across field/story/battle containers were byte-exact; a 4,000-byte growth case and rejection of malformed controls/non-ASCII targets were checked. ASCII is the current integration policy; optional punctuation mapping must be explicitly selected and is reported. Curly punctuation and accented words require a future encoding/font policy before those rows can be integrated.
